# Add/Edit Solutions in Solutions Management

Solutions highlight your organization’s expertise and the problems you help solve.

## Steps to Add or Edit Solutions

## Additional Notes 📌

Navigate to Solutions Management:

Click My Org in the main navigation bar.

Open the Offerings section and select the Solutions sub-navigation page.

Add a New Solution:

Click the Add Solution button.

Fill Solution Information:

Enter the Solution Name and a brief Summary to capture attention.

Use the AI content generator to craft the solution description based on the solution name and organization details.

Categorize the solution under your organization’s Services for better organization.

Optionally, upload Gallery Images to visually enhance your solution profile.

Save the Solution:

Click Save to finalize your solution details.

Add More Sections:

You can enhance your solution by adding details such as:

People involved

Related Case Studies

Relevant Skills and Industries

Edit & Delete Solutions:

Use the action menu (three dots on the service card) to update or remove a solution.
