# How to Resend Invitation Email

Step-by-step guide for Admins on resending invitation emails in Collective OS for seamless onboarding.

## Overview

## Step-by-Step Guide

## Key Points

Resending an invitation email in Collective OS is sometimes necessary when the original email is not received, lost, or deleted. This guide provides a step-by-step process for Admins to ensure users can promptly access and benefit from Collective OS without delays.

Step 1: Navigate to User Management

Select the Users button from the side menu bar.

The User Management module allows you to oversee all user-related information and profiles within your organization.

Step 2: Click on Action Button (Three Dots Button)

Click on the three dots action button for the user who you want to send the invitation email

Action button displays &#x27;Resend Invitation&#x27; button.

Step 3: Resend Invitation

Click Resend Invitation Email button, it will hop up the confirmation modal

Confirm the action when prompted to initiate the email resend.

Immediate Action: The invitation email is resent immediately. Inform the user to check their inbox and spam/junk mail folders.

Verification: Verify the email address for typos or errors before resending.

Support and Troubleshooting: If the email isn’t received after resending, contact Collective OS support for further assistance.
