## Key Notes

Gaining insights into other organizations on Collective OS fosters stronger collaboration and strategic partnerships. This guide helps you easily access detailed information about fellow organizations, equipping you with the tools to expand your professional network.

Step 1: Log In

Sign into your Collective OS account.

Step 2: Navigate to Main Navigation Bar

Click the Search dropdown located on the left side of the screen.

Step 3: Open Organization List

Select Organizations from the search dropdown to view the full list of organizations.

Step 4: Search and Filter

Use the search bar and filters to find specific organizations or refine your search criteria.

Step 5: Access Details

Click on an organization’s card to open their Organization Details page.

The Organization Details page is a powerful resource for understanding an organization&#x27;s scope, talents, and expertise. It allows you to explore their case studies, evaluate their skills and services, and contact representatives directly.

This feature is essential for building informed connections, scouting new clients, finding specific expertise, or initiating collaborative projects. Leveraging this functionality will help you build a robust and strategic network within Collective OS.
