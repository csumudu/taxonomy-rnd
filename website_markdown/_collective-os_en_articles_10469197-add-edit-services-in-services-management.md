# Add/Edit Services in Services Management

Easily manage your organization&#x27;s service offerings to showcase your capabilities and attract potential clients.

## Steps to Add or Edit Services

## Additional Notes 📌

Navigate to Services Management:

Click My Org in the main navigation bar.

Open the Offerings section and select the Services sub-navigation page.

Add a New Service:

Click the Add Service button.

Fill Service Information:

Enter the Service Name (up to 68 characters).

Add a brief Service Description (up to 185 characters).

Optionally, use the AI content generator to:

Generate a service description based on the service name and organization details.

Suggest relevant tags for better categorization (up to 8 tags).

Preview the service card to see how it will appear on your organization&#x27;s profile overview page.

Save the Service:

Click Save to finalize or Save and Add Another to continue adding more services.

Max 10 Limits:

Each organization can have up to 10 services.

Draft or Publish:

Save services as a Draft to work on them later or Publish them to make them live on your organization profile.

Edit & Delete Services:

Use the action menu on a service card to update or remove a service.
