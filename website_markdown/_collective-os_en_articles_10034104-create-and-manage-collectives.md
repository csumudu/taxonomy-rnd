# Create and Manage Collectives

This article provides a step-by-step guide on creating, managing, and optimizing collectives for effective collaboration and engagement.

## Steps to Create a Collective:

Navigate to Collectives Listing:

Click Collaborate in the main navigation bar

Select Collectives from the dropdown menu

Create a New Collective:

Click the Create a New Collective button

Fill Collective Information:

Type the collective name, description, and keywords, and upload the thumbnail

Choose the collective settings:

Private Collective = Only the partners of the collective can view and creator of the collective can invite to join other partners.

Vendor Collective = Only organizations in the collective can view but cannot view other organizations. creator of the collective can invite to join other organizations.

Invite Partners:

Click the Invite Partners button

Share Contents:

Share content including experts, case studies, clients & solutions within the collective.

Additional Notes:

Only collective owners can edit collective settings or manage memberships.

Members can contribute share resources, and collaborate within the guidelines set by the collective owner.
