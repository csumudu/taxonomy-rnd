# Add/Edit Organization Information in Settings

Keeping your organization information up-to-date ensures accurate representation and helps potential partners connect with you

## Steps to Add or Edit Organization Information

## Additional Notes 📌

Navigate to Settings:

Click Settings in the main navigation bar.

Open the Edit Screen:

Click the Edit Organization Information button.

Fill or Update Information:

Update details such as:

Business Name and Industry

Organization Bio: Provide a concise, easy-to-understand description (up to 380 characters).

Address and Location: Add your country, state, city, and postal code.

Tax Information: Optionally, check &quot;I have an EIN&quot; to add relevant tax details.

Save Changes:

Once all updates are complete, click Save to confirm.

Organization Bio:

A clear, concise description helps potential partners find you and understand your business focus more effectively.

Organization Representative:

Assign or update the Admin Representative who will receive key communications, such as opportunity responses and contact inquiries.
