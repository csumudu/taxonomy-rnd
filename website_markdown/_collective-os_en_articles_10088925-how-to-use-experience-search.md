# How to Use Experience Search

Guide to using Experience Search in Collective OS to efficiently find and filter relevant professional content.

## Overview

## How to Use Experience Search

## Key Points to Remember

Experience Search in Collective OS allows users to efficiently find and filter relevant content based on specific criteria. Whether you&#x27;re seeking talents with particular skills, industry-specific case studies, or clients in specific markets, this feature helps streamline your search process and uncover valuable insights.

Step 1: Accessing Experience Search

Navigate to the Experience Search section via the main navigation menu or the user profile submenu.

Click on Experience Search to access the search interface.

Step 2: Applying Filters

Upon accessing the search interface, you’ll see default filters like industry and organization.

Click the plus button to expand and access additional filters such as collective, client, case studies, skills, talents, and more.

Select desired filter values from the dropdown menu or input field to narrow down results.

Step 3: Keyword Search

Use the keyword search bar to refine results further.

Enter keywords related to your search, such as specific skills, client names, or case study summaries.

The system will prioritize results based on keyword relevance across different content sections.

Step 4: Reviewing Search Results

After applying filters and entering keywords, review the displayed results.

Results will include relevant talents, case studies, clients, and other information matching your criteria.

Step 5: Refining Search

Further refine results by using associated components like Associated Skills, Associated Clients, Associated Talents, and Associated Case Studies.

Narrow down to the most relevant information based on your needs.

Utilize filters like industry, organization, collective, client, case studies, skills, and talents to narrow results effectively.

Apply keyword search to pinpoint specific content based on skills, clients, talents, or case studies.

Carefully review and refine search results using associated components to find the most relevant information.

Regularly update search criteria and filters to discover new insights and opportunities.
