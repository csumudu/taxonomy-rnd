# Add/Manage Skills in Your Profile

Highlighting your skills increases visibility within the Collective OS network.

## Steps to Add/Manage Skills

## Available Skill Types 🛠️

Access Your Profile:

Click Profile in the main navigation bar.

Select Skills from the sub-navigation menu.

Open Skill Management:

If you’re a first-time user, click Add Skills.

Returning users can click Manage Skills.

Search and Select Skills:

Use the search bar to find specific skills or browse through categories like Skills, Industries, Markets, or Languages.

Select the skills that best represent your expertise.

Save Your Updates:

Once you&#x27;ve selected your skills, click Save to update your profile.

Skills: Specific technical or soft skills (e.g., Product Marketing, Python, Agile Methodologies).

Industries: Fields where you have experience (e.g., Technology, Education, Food Services).

Markets: Geographic regions where you have in-market experience.

Languages: Languages you’re proficient in.
