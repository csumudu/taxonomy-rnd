# How to Use Partnership Matchmaking

Guide to using Partnership Matchmaking in Collective OS to discover and connect with ideal business partners.

## Overview

## How to Use Partnership Matchmaking

## Key Points

The Partnership Matchmaking feature in Collective OS helps you efficiently discover and connect with ideal business partners. By analyzing your organization’s profile and partnership preferences, it matches you with organizations whose strategic goals, skills, and industry focus align closely with yours. This tool simplifies identifying and initiating collaborations, ensuring meaningful connections that complement your business objectives.

1. Access the Matchmaking Tool:

Log into your Collective OS account and navigate to your Dashboard.

2. Engage with Recommendations:

Review the recommended organization&#x27;s card, which includes key details like the logo, brief overview, and reasons for the match (e.g., purpose, skills, industry compatibility).

3. Use the Actionable Buttons:

Reach Out Now: Opens a communication panel to send a customized message directly to the organization.

Invite to a Collective: Sends an invitation for them to join your collective, fostering shared growth and collaboration.

4. Refine Your Matches:

If a recommendation doesn’t align with your needs, select Not Interested to remove it and refine future suggestions.

Update your preferences in the Networking Preference Questionnaires under organization settings for better-tailored future recommendations.

Dynamic Recommendations: Matches are refreshed every 72 hours, adapting to network changes and your organization&#x27;s evolving profile.

Customization and Personalization: Customize outreach messages to clearly communicate your collaborative goals.

Follow-Up and Engagement: Proactively follow up on initiated contacts to enhance partnership success, even though direct engagement tracking isn&#x27;t available.
