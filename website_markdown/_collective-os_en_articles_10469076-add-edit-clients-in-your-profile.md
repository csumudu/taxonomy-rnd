# Add/Edit Clients in Your Profile

Up-to-date client list could enhance your professional credibility and discoverability.

## Steps to Add or Edit Clients

## Additional Notes 📌

Navigate to Clients:

Click Profile in the main navigation bar.

Select Clients from the sub-navigation menu.

Add a New Company:

Click the Add Company button.

If the company is already in your organization’s client list, select it from the dropdown suggestions.

Enter Client Details:

Start typing the company name.

Select the name from the suggestions to auto-populate the company logo and website URL, or enter details manually.

Save Your Updates:

Click Save to finalize your changes.

Edit Clients: To edit a client, click the three-dot menu on the client card and select Edit.

Remove Clients: You can remove a client by clicking the three-dot menu and selecting Remove.
