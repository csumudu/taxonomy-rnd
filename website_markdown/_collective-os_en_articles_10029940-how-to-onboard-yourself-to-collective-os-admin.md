# How To Onboard Yourself to Collective OS (Admin)

Follow this guide to set up your Admin account and organization details on Collective OS.

## How To Steps:

Receive Your Invitation

Check your email for an invitation to Collective OS.

Click the link to begin setup.

Create Your Password

Use the link to set a secure password for your account.

Enter Basic Information

Fill in your name, address, and phone number.

Update Organization Information

Fill in your organization address and partnership preference questionnaire.

Explore the Dashboard

Follow the profile completion tracker to complete the ToDo items.
