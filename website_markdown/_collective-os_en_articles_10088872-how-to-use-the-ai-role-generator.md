# How to Use the AI Role Generator

Guide to using the AI Role Generator for creating detailed professional profiles from CVs or LinkedIn exports

## Overview

## How to Use the AI Role Generator

## Key Points to Remember

The AI Role Generator in Collective OS simplifies the creation of professional roles by automating the process with AI technology. It generates detailed, customized roles from uploaded CVs or LinkedIn exports, helping you strategically position your team within the network.

Step 1: Access the AI Role Generator

Log into your Collective OS account.

Navigate to Profile - Roles and find the AI Role Generator section.

Step 2: Specify Role Details

Enter the desired role title to help the AI tailor the content for the specific job title.

Step 3: Upload Documents

Select Upload CV and upload the CV or LinkedIn export file for content generation.

Step 4: Generate the Profile

Click Generate Role after uploading the document. The AI will process the information to create a profile, highlighting relevant tags, work experiences, skills, and industries.

Step 5: Review and Customize

Review the generated role content. Edit any details to better align with your or your team members&#x27; skills and expertise.

Regular Updates: Keep roles current by regularly uploading the latest CVs or LinkedIn exports and regenerating profiles.

Monthly Usage Limit: The AI Role Generator has a monthly usage limit of 5, which resets on the first day of each month.
