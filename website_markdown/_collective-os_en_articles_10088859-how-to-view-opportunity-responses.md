## Step-by-Step Guide

## Key Features

The &quot;Opportunity Response&quot; feature in Collective OS streamlines the process of managing and reviewing all responses to your posted opportunities. This centralized interface allows users to efficiently track and assess interactions, providing clear insights into each response.

1. Access Responses:

Navigate to the Opportunities Management module.

Select the opportunity you want to review.

2. View Responses:

Click on the Responses tab to see a list of all responses from various organizations.

3. Identify Response Type:

Use the navigation panel on the left to filter responses by type—such as applied, recommended, or more information requested.

4. Review Details:

Select a specific response to view in-depth information, including the organization’s name, logo, industry, and response type.

5. Interact with Responses:

In the detailed view, you can read the message from the responder.

Choose to reply or decline the pitch directly from this page.

Quick Identification: Each response card highlights key details, enabling you to quickly gauge the relevance of each response.

Direct Communication: Use built-in communication tools to respond or decline proposals efficiently, fostering smoother interactions.

Centralized Management: All opportunity-related responses are easily accessible in one place, simplifying response management.

Deep Dive into Profiles: Clicking on any expert, case study, or organization card within the response details takes you directly to their profile, offering deeper insights into their background and expertise.

By leveraging the Opportunity Response feature, you can streamline your response review process, ensuring you stay organized and responsive to all potential collaborations.
