# How to Add Services

Step-by-step guide to adding and managing services in your organization profile for better visibility.

## Overview

## Step-by-Step Guide

## Key Points to Remember

Adding services to your Collective OS profile helps communicate your organization’s capabilities clearly, boosting your profile&#x27;s appeal and discoverability within the network. Follow these steps to efficiently add services.

1. Access the Services Management Module:

Log into your Collective OS account.

Navigate to the My Org menu on the main dashboard.

Select Services from the list.

2. Create a New Service:

Click the Add New Service button at the top of the Services Management page.

A form will appear for entering service details.

3. Enter Service Details:

Service Title: Enter a concise title (max 65 characters). Keep it direct and descriptive.

Service Description: Provide a detailed description (up to 185 characters), highlighting the service’s benefits and key information.

4. Preview Your Service:

As you enter details, view a live preview of the service card.

Adjust the information as needed for optimal presentation.

5. Save and Publish:

Once satisfied, click Save to add the service to your profile.

The service will be immediately searchable within the Collective OS network, increasing your visibility.

Consistency and Clarity: Ensure the service details align with your organization’s branding and communicate value effectively.

Regular Updates: Keep service listings current to reflect any changes or enhancements.
