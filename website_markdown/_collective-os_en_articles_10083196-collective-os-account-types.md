### Custom Plans

Collective OS Plans were built to accomodate the widely varied needs of the professional services industries.  Whether you represent a global organization which is part of a larger portfolio, or whether you&#x27;re a one or two person shop - our account plans exist to provide tangible value to everyone.

For smaller organizations looking to solely connect with their existing, immediate network we offer our Free experience.  With basic features and functionality, our Free users are able to create and join limited numbers of Collectives.  You can easily view and share content within those relationships, but not have access to the larger Collective OS network.

Free Accounts are able to create and share opportunities with the larger Collective OS network.  This feature can be especially useful for organizations looking to quickly network with Partners who can help fill a need.

The Collective OS Essential, Growth and Scale plans give you access to the Public Collective OS network to find Partners and build relationships.  These plans also allow a User to browse opportunities and be notified when a new, relevant opportunity is created.

Each plan provides increasing levels of benefits, including number of users, collectives created/joined, opportunities an organization can respond to, and content to be created.  Additionally, our paid plans get access to our growing suite of AI-enabled automation services that make it easy to build content, add users and represent your organization with a few clicks.

For those with more unique needs or larger accounts, our team is able to customize our plans to be better tailored for you.  Whether you&#x27;re looking for more of a specific feature, whether you&#x27;re looking to bring along multiple agencies, or whether you&#x27;re looking for more hands-on support; are Custom Plan is available.
