# Add/Edit Professional Background in Your Profile

Your professional background showcases your career journey and expertise, making it a vital part of your profile.

## Steps to Add or Edit Professional Background

## Additional Notes 📌

Navigate to Profiles:

Click Profile in the main navigation bar.

Select Profiles from the sub-navigation menu.

Start Adding a Background:

Click the Create a Background button to begin.

Create Your Background:

Job Title: Select the job title that best represents your role (maximum 100 characters).

Description: Write a simple, concise, and accurate description of your responsibilities or achievements.

Save Your Updates:

Once all details are entered, click Save to finalize your professional background.

Edit Professional Background:

Use the three-dot menu next to the title to update any information.

Delete Professional Background:

The same menu allows you to delete a background if it’s no longer relevant.
